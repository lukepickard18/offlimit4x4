About Off Limits 4x4

Just three mates who love getting out in the 4WD, camping under the stars, fixing our rigs, and seeing where the tracks lead.

What We Do

We head off-road whenever we can — chasing tracks, finding campsites, and seeing how far we can push both ourselves and our vehicles.

If something breaks, we fix it. If we get stuck, we laugh about it later.

Why Off Limits 4x4
