About ForgedX

Just three mates who love getting out in the 4WD, camping under the stars, fixing up our rigs, and sharing the adventure.

What We Do

We hit tracks, explore trails, and camp wherever we can. Along the way, we tackle repairs, mods, and the challenges that come with real 4WD adventures.

No polished marketing, no showroom builds — just us, our mates, and the vehicles we drive hard and fix ourselves.

Why ForgedX

We just want to document the fun, the mess-ups, the breakdowns, and the fixes — showing that adventure is for everyone willing to get their hands dirty.
